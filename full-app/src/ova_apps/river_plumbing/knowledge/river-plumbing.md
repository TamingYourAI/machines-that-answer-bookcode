# River Plumbing, what callers ask

River Plumbing is the book's small example: a two-van plumbing and heating company. Everything
here is invented.

## When are you open?

The office answers Monday to Friday, eight to five. Visits run Monday to Saturday. For an emergency, you can reach the owner at any hour.

## Are you open on Saturday?

Yes, visits run on Saturday. The office itself is closed at the weekend, and this line takes bookings at any hour.

## Are you open on Sunday?

We don't make visits on Sunday. For an emergency, this line puts you through to the owner at any hour.

## How much is a call-out?

The call-out charge is sixty dollars. It is taken off the bill if the job goes ahead.

## What do you charge for a drain unblock?

The price depends on what the plumber finds. There is a sixty-dollar call-out charge, and the plumber gives you a price before starting any work.

## What work do you do?

We fix leaks, blocked drains, toilets, taps, and water heaters, and we service boilers. We don't do gas appliance repairs; for a gas smell, call the gas emergency line.

## Which areas do you cover?

We cover the city and the suburbs within about twenty miles of the river. If you give me your address, I can check it when I book.

## How do I pay?

You can pay the plumber by card or bank transfer when the job is done.

## What should I do about a burst pipe?

Turn off the water at the main stopcock if you can, then tell me it's an emergency and I'll put you through to the owner.

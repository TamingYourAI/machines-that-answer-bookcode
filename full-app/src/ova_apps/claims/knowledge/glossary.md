# Insurance words, explained

## What is a deductible?

A deductible is the part of a claim you pay yourself. For example, with a five hundred dollar deductible and a two thousand dollar repair, the payment is fifteen hundred dollars.

## What is a declarations page?

The declarations page is the first page of your policy. It lists your coverages, your limits, your deductibles, and the vehicles or property the policy includes.

## What is an adjuster?

An adjuster is the claims specialist who reviews your claim, decides what the policy pays, and arranges the payment.

## What is first notice of loss?

First notice of loss is the first report of a claim, like the one you can make on this line.

## What is subrogation?

Subrogation is when we recover what we paid from the person who caused the damage, or from their insurer.

## What is actual cash value?

Actual cash value is what something was worth just before the damage, which is the cost to replace it minus wear and age.

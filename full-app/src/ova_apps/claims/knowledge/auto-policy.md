# Auto policy booklet, plain-language summary

This summary is part of the invented Quillmere Mutual demonstration. The amounts that apply to a
particular policy are on its declarations page.

## What does collision coverage include?

Collision coverage pays to repair your car after it hits another vehicle or an object, whoever was at fault. Your collision deductible applies.

## What does comprehensive coverage include?

Comprehensive coverage is for damage that is not a collision: theft, vandalism, fire, hail, falling objects, and hitting an animal such as a deer. Your comprehensive deductible applies.

## Does my policy include windshield and glass repair?

Glass damage is part of comprehensive coverage. With the full glass option, a windshield repair or replacement has no deductible.

## Does my policy include a rental car?

If your policy has rental reimbursement, it pays for a rental car while your car is being repaired after a claim, up to the daily amount and the number of days on your declarations page.

## What does roadside assistance include?

Roadside assistance includes towing to the nearest qualified repair shop, jump starts, lockouts, flat tire changes, and fuel delivery. It is available at any hour if your policy includes it.

## What is liability coverage?

Liability coverage pays for injury to other people and damage to their property when you are responsible for an accident, up to the limits on your declarations page.

## What is not included?

The policy does not pay for wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or damage from using the car for paid deliveries or ride sharing without the matching endorsement.

## What is a total loss?

A car is a total loss when repairing it would cost more than it is worth. The payment is based on the car's actual cash value, less your deductible.

# Claims questions and answers

Quillmere Mutual is an invented insurer for the Open Voice Agents demonstration. Every answer is
written to be read aloud: one idea per sentence, the answer first.

## How do I report a claim?

You can report a claim on this line at any hour, in the app, or on our website. It takes about three minutes, and you get a claim number at the end of the call.

## What happens after I report a claim?

An adjuster is assigned to your claim and calls you, usually by the end of the next business day. They review your photos and the details, and they tell you what happens next.

## How long does a claim take?

Most car claims with photos are settled within two weeks. Claims that need an inspection or a police report can take longer, and your adjuster will tell you the next date.

## How do I send photos?

We text you a secure link after you report the claim, and you can add photos from your phone's camera. In the app, you can also use the camera button during the call.

## What photos should I take?

Take the whole damaged area from a few steps back, then close ups of each damaged part. For a car, include the license plate and the odometer. For a home, include the room and the source of any water.

## Do I need a police report?

For a theft, a break-in, or a collision with another vehicle, a police report helps. Report the claim now, and give your adjuster the report number when you have it.

## Who is my adjuster?

Your adjuster is the claims specialist who handles your claim from start to finish. Their name is in your claim status, and they call you after the claim is reported.

## Can I choose my repair shop?

Yes, you can choose any licensed repair shop. Your adjuster can also suggest shops that work with us directly.

## When do I pay the deductible?

You pay the deductible to the repair shop when the work is done, or it is taken off the payment we send you.

## Will a claim change my premium?

That depends on the claim and on your state's rules. Your agent can explain how a claim may affect your renewal.

## What if the other driver was at fault?

Report the claim to us anyway. If the other driver was at fault, we can seek payment from their insurer, and your deductible may be returned to you.

## Is my claim covered?

Whether a claim is covered is decided by your adjuster, after they review the details and your policy. I can tell you what your policy includes, but not whether a particular claim will be paid.

## How do I check my claim status?

You can call this line, use the app, or open the status link we text you. Have your claim number or your policy number ready.

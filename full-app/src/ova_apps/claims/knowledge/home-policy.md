# Home policy booklet, plain-language summary

This summary is part of the invented Quillmere Mutual demonstration. The limits that apply to a
particular policy are on its declarations page.

## What does the dwelling coverage include?

Dwelling coverage pays to repair or rebuild the structure of your home after damage from events the policy names, such as fire, wind, hail, and sudden water damage.

## Does my home policy include water damage?

The policy includes sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe. It does not include damage from a leak that went on over time, or from flooding.

## Does my home policy include flood?

A standard home policy does not include flood. Flood insurance is a separate policy.

## What are additional living expenses?

If your home cannot be lived in after a claim, additional living expenses pay for a hotel and meals above your normal costs, up to the limit on your declarations page.

## What does personal property coverage include?

Personal property coverage pays for your belongings, such as furniture, clothes, and electronics, after damage or theft, less your deductible.

## What should I do right after water damage?

Turn off the water at the main valve if you can, move valuables out of the water, and take photos before anything is thrown away. Keep receipts for any emergency repairs.

      hears:   "One moment.", then a person
      outcome: handed over; the booking left to a person
   model, mid-call
      hears:   "One moment." once; later answers a little slower
      outcome: booked by the agent

3. four failure classes: what the caller loses
   a provider swapped for the next call: no dead air
      nothing for new calls; calls in progress keep the old one
   a worker restarted mid-call: 1.8 s of dead air
      nothing, if the state was saved before the turn
   a speech session rebuilt mid-call: 2.5 s of dead air
      the audio context: the caller repeats the last answer
   a carrier or a region lost: the call drops
      the call itself; a verified callback follows, with the operations in
      flight reconciled first

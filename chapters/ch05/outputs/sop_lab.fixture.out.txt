2. the SOP compiled: meter fault, book a technician visit
   step  block     touch              source
   1     greet     -                  document
   2     identify  read crm           document
   2a    verify    read outage map    practice  <- drift: not in the document
   3     capture   write ticket       document
   4     escalate  human              document
   5     collect   read calendar      document
   5a    escalate  human              practice  <- drift: not in the document
   6     act       write booking      document
   7     confirm   write text         document
   8     close     write disposition  document
   touches: 3 reads, 4 writes, 2 human steps; drift: 2 steps

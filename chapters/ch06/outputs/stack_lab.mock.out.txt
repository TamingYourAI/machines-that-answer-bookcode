stack lab, mode=mock; evidence class: simulated (stand-in timings)

1. the contracts: every layer, the same four rules
   layer             in                 out
   transport         caller audio       20 ms frames; audio out; call control
   hearing           20 ms frames       partials, then a final with timestamps
   turn-taking       frames, partials   end of turn; barge-in
   thinking          final text, state  tokens and tool calls, streamed
   speaking          text for the ear   audio chunks, each tagged with a line
   systems of record an operation       a result, or a failure class
   orchestration     every event above  cancels; the event timeline
   observability     every span         one trace per call, on one clock
   rules: streams its output; stops within 50 ms of a cancel;
          stamps the call clock; classifies its errors
   hearing, stand-in A   passes
   speaking, stand-in B  fails: cannot be stopped in time
   thinking, stand-in C  fails: vendor timestamps only; errors are free text
   speaking, stand-in D  fails: waits for the whole reply

2. where time goes: one turn, call clock (ms)
   layer        span                                           ms
   transport    carrier and jitter buffer, in                  40
   turn-taking  caller stops speaking -> end of turn decided  220
   hearing      end of turn decided -> final transcript        40
   thinking     final transcript -> first token               170
   speaking     first token -> first audio byte                85
   transport    first audio byte -> audio leaves the server    10
   transport    carrier and jitter buffer, out                 45
   voice to voice at the caller: 610 ms
   the same boundaries in each vendor's own timestamps:
   hearing      end of turn decided -> final transcript       125
   thinking     final transcript -> first token                85
   speaking     first token -> first audio byte               -35  impossible

3. the event timeline: a barge-in 3300 ms into the fee line
   "The first opening is Tuesday at nine.
     There's a forty-dollar call-out fee. Shall I book it?"
   pipeline: played 46 of 91 characters; heard: slot
   the caller's yes covers slot only; the agent reads the fee again
   speech-to-speech: 3300 ms of audio played; the words behind
   them come from a side transcript; the terms heard are an estimate
